Sol Backups

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

This policy describes the personal-use Sol Backups Google Drive integration, operated by prodzaha. It is not a privacy policy for every component of Sol.

Data accessed and purpose

The integration uses Google OAuth authorization and the drive.file permission to upload, list, read and manage its backup repository files and their metadata in the authorized account's Google Drive. This includes temporary repository lock files. Its purpose is encrypted backup, integrity checking and restoration, not general access to unrelated Drive files.

Storage and protection

restic processes the selected source data on operator-controlled devices and encrypts repository contents before rclone uploads them to Google Drive. Backup contents may be restored to an operator-controlled device. Encryption does not hide all service metadata, such as object sizes and transfer times, from the storage provider.

OAuth credentials and recovery secrets are kept on operator-controlled devices, separate from this public documentation site. Google account passwords are not supplied to this integration. No credentials, backup contents or recovery passwords are published on this site or in its source repository.

Sharing and use

Backup data is not sold, used for advertising, or supplied to AI model training through this integration. Encrypted backup objects and associated service metadata are transferred to Google Drive as needed to provide storage and recovery. The integration does not intentionally make backup files public; existing account access and sharing settings remain relevant.

Sol Backups' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Backup objects remain in the operator's Drive until the operator authorizes their removal. This policy does not promise an automatic retention or deletion schedule. Local recovery copies remain under the operator's control. Temporary repository locks may be removed after backup operations.

The account owner can revoke the integration's access using Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access; it does not itself delete existing Drive backup objects or local recovery copies. The owner controls their removal separately.

This documentation website

This site has no sign-in form, scripts, analytics or application cookies. It does not accept backup uploads. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google's services are separately governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact and changes

The operator is identified by the linked GitHub profile. Authorized users can contact the operator through the support contact displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen. Do not publish credentials or private backup data in public GitHub messages. Policy changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
